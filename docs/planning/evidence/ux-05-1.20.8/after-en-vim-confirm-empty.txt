                       │  playlist delete <name…delete a playlist (confirmation required)                          │
                       │  playlist add <name> <…add songs to a playlist                                            │
                       │  playlist remove <name…remove a song from a playlist                                      │
                       │  playlist play <name>  play a playlist                                                    │
                       │  playlist export <name…export playlist to M3U                                             │
                       │  playlist import <file…import playlist from M3U                                           │

                       │  get playlist <url> [<…download a playlist                                                │
